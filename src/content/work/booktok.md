## What it is

BookTok is "a Netflix for book reading": shelves you browse, suggestions picked for your taste, and trending titles one tap away.

## My part

I work on it with the team at Speechify. The database lives entirely in **Supabase**: **Row Level Security** keeps each reader's data their own, and **Edge Functions** keep book metadata and search indexes up to date as the catalog changes.
